Research round table followed by a round table on AI/business challenges, with Isabelle Ryl (director of the PRAIRIE institute), Alexandre Défossez (Chief Exploration Officer, Kyutai), Francis Bach (INRIA-ENS researcher), Grégoire Mialon (researcher, Llama team at Meta), Yann Lechelle (co-founder and CEO of :probabl.), Mehdi Ghissassi (CPO, AI71) and Yohann Ralle (deputy national coordinator for the government's AI strategy).

Event organized as part of Unaite, the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations, of which HEC IA is a member.
