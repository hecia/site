36-hour hackathon organized by Unaite in partnership with Y Combinator, at Stephenson Harwood (48 rue Cambon, Paris). Three tracks: Software for Agents, Robotics (OpenArm access), The Next Big DecaCorn. Overnight mentorship from YC alumni, teams of 3 to 5.

Unaite is the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations, of which HEC IA is a member.
