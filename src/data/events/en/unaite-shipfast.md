One-day hackathon (9am-8:15pm) at École 42 Paris, presented by "Just Build It!" with Unaite as co-organizer. 100 participants, teams of up to 5, over €10,000 in prizes (cash, credits, mentorship). 5 finalists selected for a 5-minute demo each.

Partners: Anthropic, Cerebras, Lovable, Windsurf.

Event organized as part of Unaite, the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations, of which HEC IA is a member.
