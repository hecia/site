On April 30, Anthropic gave investors 48 hours to submit their allocations in a round aiming to push its valuation above $900 billion, up from $380 billion in February. A more than 2x jump in three months.

If the round closes, Anthropic would overtake OpenAI's valuation, set at $852 billion in March. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley are involved in the deal, a trio of banks that signals how serious the process is. Anthropic's announced deadline: end of May.
