A California federal jury took less than two hours to throw out Elon Musk's claims against Sam Altman, Greg Brockman and OpenAI in full, ruling that Musk had waited too long to sue (a three-year statute of limitations). Musk had sought $150 billion, accusing the trio of turning a non-profit into a for-profit company for their own benefit. Musk said he would appeal, calling it a "technicality."

[Jury throws out Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI and Sam Altman — NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-elon-musk-case-verdict-rcna345655)

The same day, Anthropic announced the acquisition of Stainless, the startup behind the SDKs used by the OpenAI API, Cloudflare and Merge. An acquisition that puts Anthropic at the heart of a direct competitor's developer infrastructure.

Google I/O unveiled Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini Omni, Gemini Spark and a $100/month "AI Ultra" tier. Google is now openly positioning itself around agents that act on the user's behalf, no longer just a search engine or a conversational assistant.

The same day, OpenAI announced that an internal model had disproved a geometry conjecture that had resisted mathematicians for 80 years. Fields medalist Tim Gowers called the result "a landmark moment for AI in mathematics," a rare acknowledgment from a mathematician of his standing for a result obtained by a model.

Within the same few-day window, SpaceX filed a public S-1 and OpenAI a confidential one. Both filings mark the start of the IPO calendar that will occupy most of June. Next to watch: the pricing of SpaceX's shares, expected in the coming weeks.
