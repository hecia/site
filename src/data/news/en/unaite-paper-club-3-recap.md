Third edition of Unaite's Paper Club, with Quentin Garrido (Meta FAIR) as speaker.

On the agenda: world models and learning latent space from video. V-JEPA 2, intuitive physics understanding from self-supervised pretraining, and latent action world models for robotics and navigation planning.

The Paper Club is a recurring Unaite format, the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations of which HEC IA is a member, dedicated to reading and discussing AI research papers with researchers and practitioners.
