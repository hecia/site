Second edition of Unaite's Paper Club, with Oriane Siméoni, from Meta's DINO team, as speaker.

On the agenda: DINOv3, a self-supervised visual representation learning model, its key ideas, technical takeaways and open research directions. The evening ended with a cocktail reception.

The Paper Club is a recurring Unaite format, the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations of which HEC IA is a member, dedicated to reading and discussing AI research papers with researchers and practitioners.
