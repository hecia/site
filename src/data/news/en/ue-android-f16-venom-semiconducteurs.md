The European Commission adopted two binding decisions forcing Google to open Android to rival AI assistants: wake-word activation equivalent to "Hey Google," and access to the same system functions. Google will also have to share search data with third-party engines. A concrete application of the Digital Markets Act to on-device AI.

[Commission provides guidance to Google for AI interoperability on Android — European Commission](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/commission-provides-guidance-google-ai-interoperability-android-and-sharing-google-search-data-under-2026-07-16_en)

The same day, in the US, DARPA and the US Air Force flew a VENOM program F-16 entirely under AI control for the first time, from Eglin Air Force Base, with a safety pilot remaining on board to take over if needed. A demonstration that brings US military AI closer to real operational deployment, beyond simulators.

TSMC posted net income up 77.4% year-on-year for Q2 2026, with AI chips now accounting for 66% of its quarterly revenue. TSMC also announced an additional $100 billion for its Arizona plants. In South Korea, an $880 billion ten-year investment plan was unveiled, covering semiconductors, AI infrastructure and robotics, with Samsung and SK Hynix committing $518 billion to new plants.
