Tech giants' AI capital expenditure reached $725 billion in 2026, up from $670 billion the previous year, roughly an 8% increase. Microsoft followed up with its largest regional commitment ever: $18 billion in Australia, to make it its Asia-Pacific AI hub.

At the same time, Meta and Microsoft announced headcount reductions, of roughly 10% and 7% of affected roles respectively, partly justified by AI's ability to absorb part of the workload. A signal worth watching: the two companies investing the most in AI are also the ones cutting staff while citing that same AI.

The 2026 Stanford AI Index, the annual reference report on the state of AI (adoption, investment, research, regulation), came out shortly after and confirms the scale of the acceleration under way since the start of the year.
