SpaceX priced its IPO at $135 a share, aiming to raise $75 billion at a $1.75 trillion valuation. It is officially the largest IPO ever, ahead of Saudi Aramco in 2019, which had held the record for seven years. Shares started trading on Nasdaq the next day under the ticker SPCX.
