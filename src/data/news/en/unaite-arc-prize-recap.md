An event dedicated to frontier AGI research, organized by Unaite with support from ARC Prize.

François Chollet, creator of Keras and ARC-AGI, joined via video call. Mathïs Federico (Automatants, CentraleSupélec) presented his own work on ARC-AGI. The evening's central theme: the limits of pure transformer scaling for learning new skills the way humans do.

Unaite is the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations, of which HEC IA is a member.
