Inaugural edition of Unaite's Paper Club, with Hugo Touvron, from the Llama team at Meta FAIR, as speaker.

On the agenda: the path from the original Llama paper to Llama 4, architecture choices, training strategies, and the discovery process behind this open-weights language model.

The Paper Club is a recurring Unaite format, the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations of which HEC IA is a member, dedicated to reading and discussing AI research papers with researchers and practitioners.
