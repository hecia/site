Google held its Cloud Next '26 conference in early April. Three announcements stand out: the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, a platform for deploying AI agents in the enterprise; the 8th generation of TPU, Google's in-house chip for training and inference; and two new models, Gemma 4 (open-weight) and Deep Research Max (automated in-depth research).

In the same window, Anthropic confirmed the existence of Claude Mythos 5 without making it public or available via API. An unusual choice in a sector where every lab usually communicates as soon as a model exists, and one that got attention beyond the usual circle of AI watchers.

OpenAI (GPT-5.5), Google (Image 2.0) and Meta (Muse Spark) rounded out the picture. According to trade press, this is one of the densest model launch windows ever observed: four major labs releasing or confirming a model within the same few-day period.
