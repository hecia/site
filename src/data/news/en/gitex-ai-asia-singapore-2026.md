Along with Axel Lefèvre, we were invited to GITEX AI Asia in Singapore, as part of our research work with HEC IA at the National University of Singapore.

For those who don't know it, GITEX is one of the largest tech events in the world, bringing together startups, large corporations and innovation players around the latest advances in AI, robotics, cybersecurity and more.

![Main stage at GITEX AI Asia 2026, panel "Chips, Clouds, and Connectivity: Building Asia's AI Backbone"](/news/gitex-ai-asia-2026/main-stage-gitex.jpeg)

What struck us the most: the very concrete convergence between AI and hardware. Many demos went well beyond software: robotics, sensors, embedded systems, with AI directly integrated into the physical world.

There's a clear shift underway:
- AI is no longer confined to interfaces or digital tools
- it is starting to drive machines, operations, real-world environments

It's both impressive and a little vertiginous.

At the same time, we felt a very dynamic ecosystem that is still taking shape: many recently funded startups, sometimes with close-to-identical pitches, and a real question mark over their ability to reach sustainable profitability.

Curious to see how fast these use cases will scale.

*Hugo Roullet & Axel Lefèvre*
