GPT-5.6 (general availability on July 1, after a limited preview on June 26), Claude Sonnet 5 and Grok 4.5 all went into production in early July, within days of each other. The three labs, OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI, answered each other almost in real time, each shipping its model while the other two were already the talk of the industry. A launch pace that leaves product teams little time to evaluate one model before the next one arrives.
