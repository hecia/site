Round table organized by Unaite on the future of artificial intelligence in France, at Albert School.

Speakers: William El Sayed (Mistral AI), Anne-Claire Baschet (Mirakl), Ivan Smets (Snowflake) and Chloé Timsit (Kima Ventures). Discussion followed by a networking cocktail.

Unaite is the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations, of which HEC IA is a member.
