OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled "Jalapeño," OpenAI's first custom-designed AI chip. A logical step after months of tension over compute supply, and a signal that frontier labs are trying to reduce their reliance on Nvidia for at least part of their needs. OpenAI thus joins Google (TPU) and Amazon (Trainium) among the major players developing their own silicon rather than depending solely on external suppliers.
