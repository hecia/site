Unaite's launch conference, at the Amphithéâtre de l'Estrapade, Université Paris Dauphine-PSL.

Two round tables on the agenda: one on research, one on the business and societal challenges of AI. Speakers: Isabelle Ryl (director of the PRAIRIE institute), Alexandre Défossez (Chief Exploration Officer, Kyutai), Francis Bach (INRIA-ENS researcher), Grégoire Mialon (researcher, Llama team at Meta), Yann Lechelle (co-founder and CEO of :probabl.), Mehdi Ghissassi (CPO, AI71) and Yohann Ralle (deputy national coordinator for the government's AI strategy). The evening ended with a cocktail reception.

Unaite is the student federation uniting France's leading AI student associations, of which HEC IA is a member.
