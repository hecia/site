GitHub moved all Copilot plans from a flat subscription to usage-based billing (GitHub AI Credits). Concrete consequence for developers: an agentic coding session now consumes $30 to $40 of credits, three to four times a Pro subscriber's full monthly allowance. A pricing shift that reflects the real cost of agentic inference, far higher than that of classic autocomplete.

[AI News Today – June 5, 2026 — BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-5-2026)

The same day, Anthropic confidentially filed its own S-1 with the SEC, formally committing to its path toward an IPO. A logical step after the valuation race that started in late April.

Representatives Jay Obernolte and Lori Trahan unveiled the "Great American Artificial Intelligence Act," a 269-page bill, the most comprehensive federal AI framework proposed to date in the US. Its headline provision: a three-year preemption of state AI laws concerning frontier models, meaning the federal government would prevent states from legislating on the topic themselves for three years.
