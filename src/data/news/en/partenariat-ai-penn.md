Colin Petit and Magloire T. Guillet, for HEC IA, have teamed up with Sean Lee, for AI @ Penn (The Wharton School), to launch a transatlantic partnership between HEC Paris and the University of Pennsylvania.

Stated goal: strengthen international student cooperation and push further on AI research, understanding and entrepreneurship across the Atlantic.

On the roadmap announced by both clubs:
- a joint transatlantic AI newsletter,
- workshops co-designed by both clubs,
- applied research initiatives with tech industry players,
- AI/data consulting missions for Y Combinator startups, co-led by both clubs,
- an incubation program for hybrid Penn-HEC AI projects.
