Meta launched Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter open-weight multimodal model under an Apache 2.0 license, able to run locally on a single consumer-grade GPU. A technical choice that matters: most frontier models require professional GPU clusters, whereas Glimmer explicitly targets the individual developer or researcher without access to massive cloud infrastructure.

Zuckerberg accompanied the launch with a 6,500-word essay, "The Future is for Everyone," making the case for open-weight models and urging Washington not to restrict foreign open models. Meta also announced a $1 billion fund for communities hosting its data centers.
