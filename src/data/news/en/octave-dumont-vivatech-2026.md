Octave Dumont, CTO at Galahad and VP in charge of HEC IA Intelligence, spoke on Saturday, June 20 at VivaTech 2026, alongside Tom Wiltberger (AI Manager, Wavestone).

Session: *"From startup to CAC 40: what are the pitfalls and accelerators of AI?"*, Executive Arena, 11:30-11:55 CET.

The topic: AI is reshaping execution models from startups to large corporations, but the real challenge is no longer experimentation, it's scaling orchestration in critical business contexts without compromising control, security and reliability. The session drew on concrete experience from Wavestone and Galahad.

HEC IA congratulates Octave on this talk.
